A letter from our founder

Last December, my grandmother was diagnosed with Alzheimer’s. By the time my family understood what was happening, the signs had been there for years. I wished I had built something sooner. Foresight exists because early detection saves lives and today, it doesn’t exist at scale.

Chronic conditions evolve gradually, and traditionally these signals go unseen because there’s no structured way to capture or understand them between appointments.

I’m building Foresight to change that. So families like mine, and yours, never miss the signs again.

Tienlan SunFounder & CEO
